# Word Count Expansion Case Study: 4554 → 8046

## Context

Article: "睡觉时公司自己变好了：YC 的 AI 递归循环实战"
Source: Y Combinator YouTube video (Tom Blomfield talk, 13 min)
Target: 8000+ words (飞哥 minimum)
Initial draft: 4554 words

## Expansion Rounds

- Added more detail to story hook (expanded the "Holy Shit moment" description)
- Added control theory framing (Law of Requisite Variety)
- Added horse-to-car analogy for paradigm shift

### Round 2: 5058 → 5972 (+914)
- Expanded each of the five layers with more concrete examples
- Added "what if your company doesn't have sensors" scenario

### Round 3: 5972 → 7152 (+1180)

### Round 4: 7152 → 8046 (+894)

## Key Techniques Used

1. **Extract data points into standalone sections**: The "5x revenue per employee" data from the source became two full sections (macro trend + Token economics)

2. **Add "boundary/caveat" sections**: Each expansion round added nuance — not just more content, but more balanced content

3. **Make action advice concrete**: "Start recording" → "3-3-3 recording method" with specific daily prompts

4. **Add intro hook**: The opening "why do some companies fall apart without the boss" question was added in the final round to strengthen the reader pull

5. **Cross-domain analogies**: Control theory (requisite variety, negative feedback loops), information theory (signal-to-noise ratio), biology (autonomic systems)

## Pitfalls Encountered

- Forbidden words found in draft: 助力, 优化, 意味着什么, 本质上
- Forbidden punctuation found: 冒号, 破折号, 双引号
- Had to run cleanup pass after expansion (expansion introduced new forbidden words)

## Rule of Thumb

For a single-source article (one video, one blog post), expect:
- Initial draft: 40-50% of target word count
- Need 2-3 active expansion rounds
- Each round should add ~500-1000 words
- Expansion sources: data extraction, boundary analysis, concrete action steps, cross-domain analogies, intro hooks

Don't just "make sentences longer" — add new analytical sections that genuinely add value.
